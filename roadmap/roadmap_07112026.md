## Strategy in one line
Rebound's defensible, unoccupied position is **legally-clean role discovery + per-posting sponsorship intelligence, wrapped in an honesty-first engine that produces résumés a candidate can defend in an interview** — as employers pivot to in-person verification and away from mass-apply spam. Own honesty-as-architecture, not honesty-as-slogan.

## v0.3.0 — Sponsorship + wage-level intelligence (flagship) 🔜
*Why #1: the most urgent, most underserved, most ownable candidate problem. The 2026 wage-level-weighted H-1B lottery + $100k fee make it newly decisive, and no tool surfaces it per-posting.*
- 🔜 **Wage-level signal** from DOL FLAG / OFLC LCA data → populate the `wage_level` field (currently `unknown`) so a role's likely lottery weighting is visible.
- 🔜 Cap-exempt employer surfacing (universities/non-profits — no lottery).
- 🔜 Sponsor-aware re-ranking baked into `/rebound:discover`.
- 🧭 Date-stamped, "not legal advice" guidance; re-check fast-moving rules before shipping fee/wage logic.

## v0.7.0 — Interview prep & momentum (evidence-based) 🧭
*Why the change: a rigid daily **streak** is the wrong mechanic for a lumpy, rejection-heavy search — a broken streak drops persistence below baseline (Silverman & Barasch 2023). Use the best-evidenced nudges instead.*
- 🧭 **Spaced retrieval** of STAR stories + problems (testing effect; distributed practice).
- 🧭 **Implementation intentions** ("When I finish a mock, I'll log one gap" — d≈0.65).
- 🧭 **Cumulative small-wins** view that never resets to zero. Streaks, if any: forgiving, effort-based, no leaderboards.

### Principles that don't change
1. **Base résumé is the source of truth** — never fabricate.
2. **Honesty-first** — no claimed expertise in unused tech; and no ATS mythology or false-precision scores in our own marketing.
3. **Primary over secondary** — relevant secondary skills support, never headline.
4. **The situation is private** — sponsorship/timeline data stays in local files, never in a résumé, never sent to any job board/recruiter/career service (only the user's own AI assistant reads it, to help).
5. **Local-first data** — the datastore and all dashboard analytics live on the user's machine; we're transparent that reasoning runs through the user's AI assistant.
6. **Legally-clean discovery** — official/public APIs and user-authorized fetching only; never scrape behind a login.
